Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026. Applies to iNardi and every game made by Each Pixel Matters.

This policy explains what information our games collect, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as we can and we don't sell your data.

Who we are

Each Pixel Matters ("we", "us") is an independent game studio. For anything about your data or your account, email support@eachpixelmatters.dev.

What iNardi collects

You don't need to give us your name, email or phone number to play. When you first open iNardi, we create an anonymous account for you. It is linked to your device, not to your identity.

To run the game we store:

  • Your player name. We give you one like "Guest 4821". You can change it, and other players can see it.
  • Game progress: your level and experience, coins and pearls, the boards, checkers, dice, avatars and frames you own, cases, achievements and daily bonus days.
  • Game history and statistics: the online games you play (moves, results and stakes), wins, streaks and leaderboard positions.
  • Friends: the players you add as friends and the invites you send or receive.
  • Activity times: when you were last online, so friends can see whether you're available.

Quick-chat messages during a game are fixed emojis and phrases. They are sent to your opponent live and are not stored.

Settings like sound volume, language and the game mode you picked are kept only on your device.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect your real name, contact details, contacts list, photos or precise location.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising trackers in iNardi today. If we add ads or in-app purchases, we'll update this policy first and say what changes.

Why we use it

  • To run online games, matchmaking, leaderboards and friends.
  • To keep your progress and purchases safe and the same across sessions.
  • To prevent cheating and abuse, for example checking every move on our server.

Who processes it

Game data is stored with Supabase, the database and server provider we use to run online play. The game website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services receive basic technical information such as your IP address when your device connects to them. We don't share your game data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you play. If you ask us to delete it, we delete your account and game data within 30 days. Records of past games may stay in anonymised form, without your name or account, so your opponents' history still makes sense.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: follow the steps on Delete your account.
  • See or correct your data: email us and we'll send you what we hold or fix it.
  • Change your name at any time from your profile in the game.

Children

Our games are not made for children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes we'll also tell you in the game.
